Frequently asked questions

Infrastructure, built to last.

When does fiber make more sense than copper?

Fiber is ideal for inter-building runs, large campuses, and distances where copper cabling reaches its limits. We can assess the right backbone for your site.

Do you document and label the finished cabling?

Yes. Clean labeling, organized patching, and as-built documentation make the infrastructure easier to manage long after installation.

Can you improve an existing network closet?

Yes. We organize and rehabilitate existing closets, then build a clearer foundation for future network, camera, and access-control work.
